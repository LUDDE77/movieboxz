What MovieBoxZ is

A cinema you already own — just organised.

MovieBoxZ finds the great films that are already free and public on YouTube, and gives them a home worth browsing. When you press play, the movie opens in the official YouTube app. We don't host or stream video — we make discovery a joy.

◈ Curated, not a feed

Thousands of hand-matched titles across genres, eras and properly organised TV series — real discovery, not a search box.

◈ Browse by era

From Modern Cinema back through the 80s & 90s, the 60s & 70s, to Classic Cinema — plus High Score IMDb picks.

◈ Region-aware

We hide titles YouTube can't play where you are, so you never hit a dead end.

◈ A family corner

A dedicated Kids section of curated cartoons and family films for the whole household.

◈ Free & private

No account, no charge, and we collect no personal data. Your favourites stay on your device.

◈ Built for the living room

Fast, focus-friendly Apple TV navigation and a cinematic look designed for the big screen.
