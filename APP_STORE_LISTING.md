## Description  (2148/4000)
MovieBoxZ is a discovery guide for classic and cult film. It helps you find full-length movies and TV series that are already publicly available to watch online, and opens each one in the app that actually hosts it — today, the official YouTube app.

MovieBoxZ does not host, stream, upload or store any video. It is a browsing and curation layer: it surfaces publicly-available titles and hands playback off to the provider's own app. Think of it as a beautifully organized front door to golden-age Hollywood, film noir, spaghetti westerns, sci-fi, horror, cult oddities and classic TV — hand-matched and sorted so finding something great to watch is effortless.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT

• Curated, not a feed — hand-matched titles across genres, eras and properly organized TV series. Real discovery, not a search box.

• Browse by era — from Modern Cinema back through the 80s & 90s and 60s & 70s to Classic Cinema, plus High Score IMDb picks.

• Region-aware — we hide titles that can't play where you are, so you never hit a dead end.

• A family corner — a dedicated Kids section of curated cartoons and family films.

• Private by design — no account, no sign-up, and we collect no personal data. Your favorites stay on your device.

• Built for the living room — fast, focus-friendly Apple TV navigation and a cinematic look designed for the big screen, plus iPhone and iPad.

HOW IT WORKS

Browse or search, tap a title, and it opens in the provider's official app (today, YouTube). MovieBoxZ links out to content the provider makes publicly available — it does not host or control that content, so availability is set by the content owners and the hosting service, and a title may occasionally be unavailable in your country.

Movie and TV information and artwork are provided by TMDB and OMDb/IMDb. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Questions? support@movieboxz.com
