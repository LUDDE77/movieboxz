# Channel Affiliations — inclusion basis for newer channels

Under the "Verified affiliates of an established channel family" clause of
[CONTENT_SOURCING_POLICY.md](CONTENT_SOURCING_POLICY.md), a channel under one year
old may be indexed when it is owned by / affiliated with an established operator
whose other channel(s) are **older than two years**, share the same ownership,
branding, and catalog, and operate legitimately within YouTube's system.

This file records, for each such newer channel, the established channel(s) that
are the basis for including it.
